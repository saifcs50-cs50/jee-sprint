## Fonts (`assets/fonts/`)

Latin-subset WOFF2 files. In the Mac and Android builds they are served from the app's own `fonts/` folder. Each family is published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (<https://openfontlicense.org>).

| Family | Weights bundled | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Atkinson Hyperlegible | 400, 700 | Braille Institute of America, <https://github.com/googlefonts/atkinson-hyperlegible> |
| Barlow Condensed | 500, 600, 700 | Jeremy Tribby, <https://github.com/jpt/barlow> |
| IBM Plex Mono | 400, 500 | IBM, <https://github.com/IBM/plex> |

They are Google Fonts' latin subsets (the `unicode-range` values in `fonts.css` are Google's latin range). Each family's copyright notice and the full licence text sit next to the fonts, copied from the Google Fonts repository: `assets/fonts/OFL-atkinson-hyperlegible.txt`, `OFL-barlow-condensed.txt` and `OFL-ibm-plex-mono.txt`. The copyright fields embedded in the font files match them. IBM Plex Mono carries the Reserved Font Name "Plex".
