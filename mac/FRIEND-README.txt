JEE SPRINT: INSTALL GUIDE
=========================

A 12-week JEE Main 2027 plan and tracker: every chapter scheduled week by
week, a pace line, daily timetables, a mock-score log and study-block
reminders. There's a Mac app and an Android app, and they sync over your
home Wi-Fi. There's no account and nothing goes to the internet.


1. MAC
------
1. Double-click "JEE Sprint (Mac).zip", then drag "JEE Sprint" into your
   Applications folder.
2. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because
   it isn't from the App Store. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to JEE Sprint, then Open.
   You only need to do this once.
3. If macOS asks whether JEE Sprint may find devices on your local network,
   click Allow. That's how your phone syncs.
4. Keep the app running so your phone can sync. Closing the window is
   fine; quitting it (Cmd+Q) stops sync. To have it start with your Mac,
   go to System Settings > General > Login Items, click +, and pick
   JEE Sprint.


2. ANDROID
----------
1. Send "JEE Sprint.apk" to your phone and tap it.
2. Android asks to allow installs from that app (WhatsApp, Files, etc.).
   Allow it, then tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap
   "Install anyway". It warns because the app isn't from the Play Store.


3. PAIR THE PHONE WITH THE MAC (once)
-------------------------------------
1. On the Mac app, scroll to the bottom ("Sync and reminders"). Under
   "Phone setup" you'll see the Mac's address, like 192.168.0.10, and a
   pairing code like ABCD-2345.
2. On the phone app, open the same section at the bottom. Type in the
   address and the code, then tap "Save & sync". The numbers alone are
   enough for the address; you don't need to type http:// or :5199.
3. It should say "Last synced with your Mac just now".
The phone and the Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi.

If it says it couldn't reach your Mac:
- Check the phone is on Wi-Fi (the same one as the Mac), not mobile data.
- Check the Mac app is open.
- If the Mac asked "Do you want the application JEE Sprint to accept
  incoming network connections?", the answer needs to be Allow. You can
  change it in System Settings > Network > Firewall > Options.
- If the Mac app shows no address, find it in System Settings > Wi-Fi >
  Details (it's the "IP address").


4. REMINDERS (phone)
--------------------
At the bottom of the phone app, tap "Turn reminders on" and allow
notifications. You'll get a nudge when each study block starts. Opening the
app keeps the next 14 days of reminders topped up.


HOW SYNC WORKS
--------------
- Both apps work fully offline. Tick chapters and log mocks anywhere.
- When the phone app is open on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac (with the Mac app
  running), they swap changes. If you changed the same chapter on both,
  the most recent change wins.
- The Mac keeps your progress plus a daily backup for 3 weeks in
  ~/Library/Application Support/JEE Sprint.
- If sync stops working, your Mac's Wi-Fi address has probably changed.
  Check the address in the Mac app again and re-enter it on the phone.
